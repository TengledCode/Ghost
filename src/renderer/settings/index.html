ElevenLabs voices

For a voice that doesn't sound like every narrator: on elevenlabs.io open Voices → Voice Library (or Voice Design to make one from a description), add it to My Voices, then copy its ID here.

Lower steadiness sounds livelier and less monotone; higher is calmer and more even. Style exaggerates the voice's own character (not used by v3).

Edge voices (fallback)

Bluetooth headphones and some speakers doze off in silence and miss the first word. Ghost plays an inaudible 20 Hz tone to keep them awake, and after a long silence gives them a moment to wake before speaking.

Live screen view

While live view is on, each message includes a snapshot of the monitor under your cursor, so you can just ask "what's this error?". Ghost leaves itself out of the picture, and a red LIVE tag shows whenever it's on. You can also type "watch my screen" or "stop watching".
